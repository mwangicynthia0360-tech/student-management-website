Know more about the Cynergy School Of Psychosocial Care.

At Cynergy School of Psychosocial Care, we train practitioners to care for the whole person: their emotions, relationships, communities, and the circumstances of their lives. Our programs combine strong theory with supervised, hands-on practice in counseling, trauma-informed care, grief and loss support, child and family wellbeing, and community-based work. We teach with cultural humility, and we prepare students to serve with skill, compassion, and integrity. Our graduates go on to work in hospitals, schools, NGOs, faith-based organizations, and community programs, helping people and communities heal and thrive.

Our Mission

To equip students with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to promote emotional wellbeing and strengthen the social bonds that help individuals, families, and communities recover and thrive.

Our Vision

A world where every person, regardless of background or circumstance, has access to compassionate, competent, and culturally responsive psychosocial support and holistic care.

What We Teach

Our programs combine rigorous theory with hands-on practice. Students learn counseling and communication skills, trauma-informed approaches, grief and loss support, child and family wellbeing, community-based care, and ethical practice. Training is grounded in real-world settings, including healthcare, schools, humanitarian response, and community programs.

Core Values

  • Compassion: We care with warmth and respect.
  • Dignity: We honor the worth of every person.
  • Integrity: We practice with honesty and ethics.
  • Resilience: We sustain our own wellbeing to serve others well.
  • Holistic Care: We nurture mind, body, relationships, and spirit together.
  • Collaboration: We serve best when we work together.
  • Excellence: We pursue high standards in teaching and practice.
