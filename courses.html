  • Counseling and Communication Skills
  • Human Growth and Development
  • Ethics and Professional Practice
  • Mental Health Awareness
  • Supervised Field Placement (short)

Short Courses

Duration: 2 to 8 weeks | Entry: Open to all, with no formal qualifications required

Durations and topics are suggestions. Adjust them to fit your timetable and accreditation.

Focused, practical training for working professionals, volunteers, and community members who want to build specific skills without committing to a full program.

Introduction to Psychosocial Care (2 weeks)

The basics of emotional and social wellbeing and how to offer first-level support.

Counseling and Communication Skills (4 weeks)

Active listening, empathy, and talking with people in distress.

Mental Health Awareness (2 weeks)

Recognizing common conditions, reducing stigma, and knowing when to refer.

Trauma-Informed Care (4 weeks)

Understanding trauma and responding in ways that build safety and trust.

Grief, Loss, and Bereavement Support (4 weeks)

Supporting people through death, illness, and major life change.

Child Protection and Safeguarding (3 weeks)

Recognizing risks to children and responding safely and ethically.

Self-Care and Resilience for Practitioners (2 weeks)

Preventing burnout and sustaining long-term practice.

Psychosocial Support in Emergencies (6 weeks)

Practical approaches for conflict, displacement, and disaster settings.
